DMCA Copyright Policy & Takedown Procedure

SaveAMedia respects the intellectual property rights of creators and adheres to the Digital Millennium Copyright Act of 1998.

SaveAMedia (https://saveamedia.com) is an automated web utility that allows users to parse publicly available Instagram media URLs. We do not host, store, duplicate, or maintain a permanent catalog of third-party media files on our servers. All media data is streamed directly from public Meta/Instagram content distribution nodes to the requesting user’s browser client.

It is our strict policy to respond promptly to clear, formal notices of alleged copyright infringement that comply with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act of 1998 (17 U.S.C. § 512).

📄 Requirements for Filing a Valid DMCA Notice

To file a valid copyright infringement notification with our designated agent, you must provide a written communication containing all of the following six elements:

  1. Physical or Electronic Signature: A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.
  2. Identification of Copyrighted Work: Clear identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  3. Identification of Infringing URL: Identification of the specific material or public Instagram URL that is claimed to be infringing.
  4. Contact Information: Information reasonably sufficient to permit us to contact you, including your legal name, address, telephone number, and email address.
  5. Good Faith Statement: A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. Accuracy & Perjury Statement: A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
