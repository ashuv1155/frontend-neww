Device Guide

How to Download Instagram Reels on Any Device

Step-by-step guide for iPhone, Android, Windows PC, and Mac

📱 Download Instagram Reels on iPhone (iOS)

  1. Open Instagram, find the public Reel, tap Share → Copy Link.
  2. Open SaveAMedia in Safari (not Chrome).
  3. Paste the link and tap Download Reel.
  4. Tap the blue download circle in Safari's address bar.
  5. Open Files → Downloads, tap the video, then Share → Save Video.
  6. The Reel is now in your iPhone Camera Roll (Photos app).

🤖 Download Instagram Reels on Android

  1. Open Instagram, find the public Reel, tap Share → Copy Link.
  2. Open SaveAMedia in Google Chrome.
  3. Paste the link and tap Download Reel.
  4. Tap the primary download button for 1080p HD MP4.
  5. The file saves automatically to your Downloads folder.
  6. Open Google Photos or Gallery to find the saved Reel.

🖥️ Download Instagram Reels on Windows PC

  1. Open Instagram.com in a browser and find the Reel.
  2. Click the three dots (⋮) and select Copy link.
  3. Open SaveAMedia in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
  4. Paste the link and click Download Reel.
  5. Click Download 1080p HD MP4 and save to your PC.
  6. Find the file in File Explorer → Downloads.

🍎 Download Instagram Reels on Mac

  1. Open Instagram.com in Safari or Chrome on your Mac.
  2. Click the three dots (⋮) on the Reel and select Copy link.
  3. Open SaveAMedia in your browser.
  4. Paste the link and click Download Reel.
  5. Click Download 1080p HD MP4 to save the file.
  6. Find the MP4 file in Finder → Downloads.

Frequently Asked Questions About Downloading Instagram Reels

Common questions about SaveAMedia, Instagram Reel downloading, video quality, iOS/Android saving, and copyright.

How do I download Instagram Reels in 1080p Full HD? +

Open the Instagram app or website, tap the Share icon on your chosen public Reel or video, and copy the link. Paste the URL into the SaveAMedia input box above and tap Download. Verify the video in our live preview player and choose Download HD 1080p MP4 to save the crystal-clear file directly to your device.

Are Instagram Reel & Video downloads completely free and unlimited? +

Yes! SaveAMedia is 100% free with unlimited downloads, zero daily caps, no registration walls, and zero login requirements. We never ask for your Instagram password or account details.

Does SaveAMedia add watermarks or logos to downloaded videos? +

No. SaveAMedia streams the original master MP4 video directly from Instagram's content delivery networks. We never inject watermarks, logos, or extra compression overlays.

How do I save downloaded Reels to my iPhone Camera Roll? +

In Apple Safari (iOS 13+), tap the Download MP4 button. Tap the blue download circle in Safari's address bar, open the video in Files → Downloads, tap Share, and tap Save Video to move it directly into your Photos app (Camera Roll).

Where are downloaded Instagram videos saved on Android, Mac & PC? +

Files save directly into your device's default Downloads folder in standard .mp4 format. On Android, downloaded videos appear immediately inside Google Photos or Samsung Gallery. On PC or Mac, you can access them via File Explorer or Finder.

Can I convert or download only the audio (MP3) from an Instagram Reel? +

Yes! SaveAMedia isolates the audio stream from the video container and provides a dedicated Download Audio (MP3) option. This is ideal for extracting viral music tracks, speech bites, and trending background scores.

Can I download private Instagram videos or Close Friends stories? +

No. SaveAMedia strictly complies with creator privacy and copyright standards. Only publicly accessible Instagram Reels, videos, posts, and stories can be parsed. Content from private accounts is protected by Instagram session tokens.

Why is my downloaded Reel missing sound or audio? +

Ensure your device volume or media player is not muted. If the audio is still silent, the creator may have used a licensed commercial music track that Instagram mutes when viewed outside its official app in specific countries. For original creator audio, SaveAMedia delivers the complete audio track.

Why did my Instagram Reel link fail or show 'Media Unavailable'? +

Extraction failures usually happen if: (1) The account is set to private; (2) The Reel was deleted or archived by the author; (3) The link copied was incomplete; or (4) Instagram public servers are momentarily rate-limiting requests. Test opening the link in a private/incognito tab to ensure it is publicly visible, then retry.

Is it legal and safe to download Instagram Reels? +

Yes. Downloading public media for personal offline viewing or archiving is permitted under Fair Use. However, re-uploading, republishing, or commercially exploiting someone else's content without creator permission violates copyright law. SaveAMedia is completely safe: we do not host copies of videos or log personal browsing data.
