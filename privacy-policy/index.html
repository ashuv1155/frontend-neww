Privacy Policy

Last revised: January 2026 • Compliant with Google AdSense, GDPR & CCPA

At SaveAMedia (accessible at https://saveamedia.com), we prioritize the privacy and security of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal and non-personal information collected, how it is recorded and used, and your explicit rights under international data protection laws including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and Google AdSense publisher policies.

🔒 1. Zero Credential & Authentication Policy

SaveAMedia operates under a strict zero-credential architecture. When using our Instagram Reel Downloader:

  • We never ask for, record, or store your Instagram username or password.
  • We never request your Instagram two-factor authentication (2FA) codes or session tokens.
  • We never access your private Instagram direct messages, follower lists, or private profile media.
  • All extractions are performed strictly on publicly accessible URLs published on public Meta Content Delivery Networks.

🍪 2. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies Policy

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to saveamedia.com and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

3. Client-Side Tracking Parameter Stripping

When you paste an Instagram link into SaveAMedia, our client-side JavaScript automatically strips tracking parameters (such as ?igsh=, ?utm_source=, and session IDs) directly inside your web browser before the link is sent to our extraction service. This prevents user tracking across web sessions.

4. Server Log Files & Media Retention

Like most website operators, SaveAMedia utilizes standard log files for system performance monitoring and defense against automated attacks. These logs may include IP addresses, browser types, Internet Service Providers (ISP), date/time stamps, and referring pages. These logs are not linked to any personally identifiable information and are rotated and deleted automatically.

Media Storage: SaveAMedia does not store, host, archive, or retain any videos, photos, or audio files on our servers. Media requests are parsed directly from Instagram CDN nodes and streamed to your device client.

5. GDPR Privacy Rights (European Economic Area & UK)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA) or UK, you are entitled to the following rights under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR):

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate data.
  • The right to erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You have the right to request deletion of your personal data.
  • The right to restrict or object to processing: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data.
  • The right to data portability: You have the right to request transfer of your data to another organization.

6. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • You have the right to request disclosure of categories and specific pieces of personal data collected.
  • You have the right to request deletion of personal information collected.
  • Non-Sale of Personal Data: SaveAMedia does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for monetary or other valuable consideration.
  • You have the right not to receive discriminatory treatment for exercising your privacy rights.

7. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

SaveAMedia does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child provided personal information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will make best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.
