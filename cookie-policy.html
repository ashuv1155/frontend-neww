Cookie Policy

Last revised: January 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how SaveAMedia ("we", "us", or "our") uses them on https://saveamedia.com, and how you can manage your preferences.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites function properly, work more efficiently, and provide reporting information to operators.

⚙️ 2. Categories of Cookies We Use

A. Strictly Necessary & Functional Cookies (Essential)

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They store your theme preference (saveamedia-theme), preserve your cookie consent selection, and enforce rate-limiting protections against automated denial-of-service attacks.

B. Analytics & Performance Cookies

These allow us to count page visits and extraction traffic sources so we can measure and improve gateway stability. All data collected by these cookies is aggregated and completely anonymous.

C. Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART)

Third-party advertising vendors, including Google, set cookies to serve relevant ads to you based on your browsing patterns across this site and other websites on the Internet. These cookies also limit the number of times you see an ad (frequency capping) and measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.

👁️ 3. How to Opt Out of Advertising Cookies

You can manage or opt out of personalized third-party advertising cookies at any time using the official industry tools:

  • Google Ad Settings: https://adssettings.google.com
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA): https://www.aboutads.info/choices
  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI): https://optout.networkadvertising.org

4. Managing Cookies in Your Web Browser

Most modern web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences:

Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies > Block third-party cookies.
Apple Safari (iOS & Mac): Settings / Preferences > Privacy > Prevent Cross-Site Tracking.
Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection > Strict.
Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies.
